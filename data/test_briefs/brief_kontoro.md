# Product brief: Kontoro mobile banking app

**Brand:** Kontoro (fictional)  
**Planned campaign id in database:** 65  
**Goal:** app_installs  
**Budget:** 20,000 EUR  
**Target country:** AT  
**Timeline:** 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-19

## Product
Free mobile current account with automatic round-up savings, targeting first-time savers.

## Target audience
Adults 22-38 in Austria, early career, interested in personal finance and budgeting.

## Desired tone
educational, trustworthy, no get-rich-quick promises

## Restrictions
No influencers who promote: crypto, gambling, other banking or finance apps.
