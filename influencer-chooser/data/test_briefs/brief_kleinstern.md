# Product brief: Kleinstern Sleepy Stars Baby Sleep Sack

**Brand:** Kleinstern (fictional)  
**Goal:** sales  
**Budget:** 16,000 EUR  
**Target countries:** AT, DE (Austria and Germany)  
**Timeline:** 2026-11-16 to 2026-12-13 (four weeks, gift-guide posts in the last two weeks)  
**Platforms:** Instagram and TikTok preferred, YouTube for longer sleep-routine videos

## Product
Sleepy Stars Baby Sleep Sack, sizes 0-6 months and 6-18 months, 49.90 EUR, sold online and in baby stores in Austria
and Germany. Launch 16 November 2026, positioned as a Christmas gift for new parents. Certified organic cotton
(OEKO-TEX tested), two-way zipper for night-time diaper changes without waking the baby, TOG 1.0, developed with
midwives, safe-sleep guidelines followed, grows with the baby, machine washable at 40 degrees.

## Deliverables
Reels and TikTok videos showing a real bedtime routine, optional story set with a discount code.

## Target audience
Women aged 27-40 in Austria and Germany who are expecting a baby or have a baby or toddler. They follow parenting
content, look for honest recommendations from other parents and care about organic materials and safety. The audience
of the creators should be mostly female and based in Austria or Germany.

## Creators
The creators should be parents themselves, with a baby or toddler, or expecting a baby, so the bedtime routine is real.

## Desired tone
Authentic and casual, with real family life instead of staged perfection. Humour is welcome. No fear-based messaging
about sleep and no medical promises.

## Restrictions
No influencers who promote: alcohol, gambling, crypto. No creators who work for competing baby brands (for example
Windelwunder) around the launch. All posts must carry a clear advertising label (Werbung).
